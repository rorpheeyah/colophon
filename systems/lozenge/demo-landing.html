Questions people actually ask

Can we change plan mid-term?

Yes. A change takes effect on the next invoice and the difference is prorated to the day.

Do you charge per seat?

Seats are included up to each plan’s limit. Beyond it, the next plan is cheaper than the overage.

What happens to our data if we leave?

It exports in full, in the format it went in, for as long as the account exists.
